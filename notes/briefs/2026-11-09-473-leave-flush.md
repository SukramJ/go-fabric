## Finding

`Node.FactoryReset` → `OperationalCredentials.RemoveFabric` → `fabricLeaving` (node/node.go:531-541):
`EmitLeave`, then `Bridge.EmitFabricLeavingContext` (bridge/bridge.go:1361-1383) →
`subscription.Manager.FlushFabric` (im/subscription/manager.go:577-612). `FlushFabric` drains
the pending events and the dirty attributes of each subscription of the fabric and hands them
to **two** reporters in turn — `eventReporter` then `reporter` — each sent under `withFlushWait`
and each awaited by `awaitFlushedReport` (bridge/subscribe.go:958-995) with the chunk
StatusResponse bound `chunkStatusResponseTimeout` (subscribe.go:52-75; ~14 s on a UDP session).

The controller answers the first report (the Leave event), its `onLeave`
(controller/client/reactions.go:96-150, mirrors Peers.ts `#onLeave`) runs `Peer.Disconnect(ErrPeerLeft)`
= `HandlePeerLoss`, which gives the sessions up **without a CloseSession** (client/peer/peer.go:567-579,
matter.js Peer.ts:504-527 `disconnect` → `initiateForceClose`). The second report, the attributes,
then has no reader: it waits the full bound. That is the S9 log
(`event_report.flush_answered`, then `report.flush_unanswered timeout=14.051s`). The issue's
second option (end the wait at the peer's CloseSession) does not apply: no CloseSession is sent,
in matter.js either.

matter.js at db021da0 does both differently:

1. **One report.** `Fabric.leave` (packages/protocol/src/fabric/Fabric.ts:447-457): `#leaving.emit()`,
   then per session `initiateClose(() => session.closeSubscriptions(true))`, then `delete`.
   `NodeSession.closeSubscriptions(flush)` (packages/protocol/src/session/NodeSession.ts:312-323)
   calls `subscription.close(flushViaSession)` per subscription.
   `ServerSubscription.close` → `#close(true, …)` (packages/node/src/node/server/ServerSubscription.ts:768-770,
   :778-788): `#markClosed` (:804-813, sets `#isClosed`), then `#cancel` (:816-825):
   `#sendUpdatesActivated = false`, `#changeHandlers.close()`, then `#flush` (:753-763):
   **one** `#triggerSendUpdate(true, session)` → `#sendUpdate` (:534-556) takes
   `attributeFilter` and `eventsMinNumber` together → `#sendUpdateMessage` (:893-960) sends **one**
   DataReport whose payload is `#iterateDataUpdate(exchange, attributeFilter, eventsMinNumber)`
   (:926-936). Attributes and events travel in the same report; the peer answers once.
2. **500 ms, no retransmission.** `#isClosed` is already true during that flush, so
   `waitForAck: !this.#isClosed` (:921, :936) is **false**:
   `sendDataReportMessage(dataReport, waitForAck=false)` (packages/protocol/src/interaction/InteractionMessenger.ts:799-844)
   sends with `disableMrpLogic: true` (:840) and waits
   `waitForSuccess("DataReport", { timeout: Millis(500) })` (:844). `disableMrpLogic`
   (packages/protocol/src/protocol/MessageExchange.ts:785-798): the message keeps `requiresAck`
   (the peer still acks it) but no retransmission timer and no ack promise are set up — sent
   once, not resent, not waited for at the MRP layer.

So the leave flush in matter.js is: one DataReport per subscription with everything owed, sent
once, answered within 500 ms or abandoned. Ours is two reports, each retransmitted and awaited
up to the MRP bound. Both differences are ours to close; neither is a `by_design` row.

## Scope

### §1 One flush report per subscription (`im/subscription`, `bridge`)

- `Manager.FlushFabric` hands each subscription's drained events **and** dirty paths to **one**
  reporter call. Shape: `type FlushReporter func(ctx context.Context, sub *Subscription, events []im.EventReport, paths []im.ConcreteAttributePath)`
  and `Manager.SetFlushReporter` (Added); `Reporter` and `EventReporter` stay for the ongoing
  paths. Without a flush reporter `FlushFabric` sends nothing (the bridge always wires it, as it
  wires the other two — see where `SetEventReporter` is called).
- The bridge implements it (`Bridge.SubscriptionFlushReporter()` beside `SubscriptionReporter`/
  `SubscriptionEventReporter`, subscribe.go:218-240): factor the attribute-report building of
  `reportSubscription` (subscribe.go:463-512: subCtx stamping, `unskippedChangedPaths`,
  `readAuthorizedResults`) and the event authorization of `reportSubscriptionEvents`
  (:274-300, `authorizedEventReports`) into builders the three reporters share; the flush builds
  one `im.ReportData{HasSubscription, SubscriptionID, Reports, EventReports}` and sends it through
  `sendInitiatedReportArmed` once, then **one** `awaitFlushedReport` with op `subscribe.flush`.
  Empty after authorization → nothing sent (matter.js `suppressEmptyReport: onlyWithData`, :937).
  Error handling as the ongoing paths (requeue + `noteSubscriptionSendError`) — but note the
  subscription is about to go; keep it simple and mirror the existing two.
- Cite in the Go comments: ServerSubscription.ts:753-763, :534-556, :926-936 at db021da0;
  Fabric.ts:447-457; NodeSession.ts:312-323. Update the stale `862ad75f` / `NodeSession.ts:294-306`
  / `ServerSubscription.ts:753-763 at 862ad75f` cites in bridge.go:1361-1383, manager.go:565-576,
  node.go:531-539, subscribe.go:958-970 to the db021da0 lines above (verify each with `sed -n`).

### §2 The flush wait is 500 ms (`bridge/subscribe.go`)

- `awaitFlushedReport` waits **500 ms** (`const flushStatusResponseTimeout = 500 * time.Millisecond`,
  cite InteractionMessenger.ts:844 with the `waitForAck = !#isClosed` chain
  ServerSubscription.ts:921/:936, :804-813, :816-825) instead of `chunkStatusResponseTimeout`.
  The other two ends (answer, ctx) stay; the log keys stay (`flush_answered`, `flush_abandoned`,
  `flush_unanswered`, the latter now with `timeout=500ms`).
- Over TCP the same bound applies (matter.js's timeout is transport-independent).

### §3 The flush report is sent once, untracked (`bridge/subscribe.go`)

- Mirror `disableMrpLogic` (MessageExchange.ts:785-798): the flush report's chunks go out with
  `NeedsAck` (the peer acks) but are **not** registered with the outbound reliable tracker — no
  retransmission, no `reportCounterOwner` entry. Add a flag through `sendInitiatedReportArmed` →
  `sendReportChunksNotify` → `sendUnsolicitedIM` (subscribe.go:896, :812, :1012-1062; the tracker
  decision is at :1047-1050 and the registration below it). Verify first that the ack tracker
  tolerates an inbound ack for a counter it does not hold (find where inbound acks are processed
  in bridge/ackpump.go / bridge/receive*.go) — it must be a no-op. **Stop rule:** if the untracked
  send needs more than ~50 lines or the unknown-ack path is not a no-op, leave the send tracked,
  say so in the report, and write the difference as a `// Differs from matter.js …` comment at
  `awaitFlushedReport` (the retransmissions stop with the session's eviction, bridge/conn.go:525-540);
  I decide then whether it becomes a `by_design` row.

## Decisions (taken)

- No controller change: our `onLeave` mirrors Peers.ts `#onLeave` (decommission, force disconnect
  without CloseSession, delete); the teardown order is the expected one.
- `FlushFabric` does not close the subscription itself (matter.js's `#isClosed` precedes the flush;
  ours are closed with their sessions right after, in `EmitFabricRemovedContext`). The window
  between flush and close can only produce an ongoing, non-awaited report. Say so in the
  `FlushFabric` comment.
- No findings-register row, no `by_design` row (unless §3's stop rule fires).
