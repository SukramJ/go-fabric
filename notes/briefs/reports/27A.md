## Questions

1. Should PQC Phase 1 stay off for hosts until a released Matter revision carries it (as matter.js), or get a host opt-in now? Recommendation: keep it off.
2. The controller now rotates its operational key on every start (matter.js's default). Keep it without an opt-out, or add `Config` opt-out? Recommendation: keep, add an opt-out only on a host's request.
3. circl was the decided dependency; `crypto/mldsa` of Go 1.27 does the same with none. Confirm the switch.
