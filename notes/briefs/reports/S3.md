## Differences from the brief

1. The API (`Level`, `LevelSet`, accessors) is hand-written in
   `schema/levels.go`; `levels_gen.go` holds the data and the hash.
2. `mandatory` in the level files also counts `Rev >= vN` with N at most the
   cluster's revision (BasicInformation.ConfigurationVersion at 6), the
   brief's "unconditionally mandatory at that revision".
3. The mandatory keep reason is the chain's rewrite, `kept: mandatory at pin
   (absent at level <L>)`, not `kept: mandatory at pin, absent at level <L>`.
4. `device.New` returns the withheld device **with** the error (no servers,
   its report); the generated typed constructors do too (devicegen template,
   91 files regenerated). `WithheldError` carries `Report`.
5. The root: its clusters are candidates (`Node.Surface()` gains 21 rows at
   every level). A root cluster is mandatory when RootNode's requirement may
   mandate it (`M`, or a condition): treating Groupcast ("GroupcastListenerCond,
   GroupcastSenderCond, O") as optional removed it below 1.6.0 and the
   device-type validator then reported `RootNode requirement=Groupcast
   kind=missing` (measured), against ADR 0030 decision 3. The root is never
   withheld; it keeps such a cluster as mandatory at the pin.
6. An element no level knows (`Levels == 0`, a vendor's) is not
   level-controlled.
7. The D.9 rule is in the policy (tested) but never fires in the daemon:
   BridgedDeviceBasicInformation is assembler-mounted, no candidate (S1
   difference 1), so ConfigurationVersion is served at every level.
8. The server-internal ledger also covers the Aggregator endpoint; its rows
   and the root's optional clusters carry `OriginKitDefault`.

## Decisions taken

- A level naming the pin digests and composes as the zero value.
- Commands are one id list per cluster, request and response alike.
- The daemon logs a withheld device at WARN (`surface.withheld`, keys `key`,
  `device_type`, `spec_level`) and adds the rest.

## Left out

Behaving like an older revision, levels below 1.4, profiles, digest
persistence, a `CHIP_TEST_IMAGE` lift (as briefed). No chip-tool fixture
run: the default composition's effective surface is unchanged.

3. Root clusters the pin *may* mandate are kept (Groupcast below 1.6.0) — or
   evaluate the hosted device types' conditions instead?
4. Reason text: the brief's literal string or the chain's rewrite?
5. D.9 needs the assembler's clusters in the surface (S1 Q1) to bite.
6. Is a non-nil device beside `ErrWithheld` the API you want, or a
   `node.Withhold(*WithheldError)` instead?
