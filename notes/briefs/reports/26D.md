## Differences from the brief and the read map

- The datastore has 20 commands at the pin (`joint-fabric-datastore-server.cpp:293-785`), not 24.
- The `DatastoreSync` port is synchronous. chip's two failure points, a delegate call refused at its start and a completion with an error, collapse into one. It is handled as chip handles the completion: the entry stays Pending, a per-entry push of the refresh goes on, and a failed fetch or whole-list push ends the refresh. chip's endpoint-scoped `SyncNode(nodeId, endpointId, bindings)` overload is not in the port, because the datastore never calls it.
- `RefreshNode` of a node that is not held ends the refresh. chip leaves `mRefreshingNodeId` set on that path (:247-249, :262), and every later refresh is then refused with INCORRECT_STATE.
- **The refresh's binding merge removes only the refreshed endpoint's missing bindings.** chip's predicate (:567-585) has no endpoint filter and drops the node's committed bindings of every other endpoint while it processes one endpoint, so after a refresh only the last endpoint's bindings remain. The groups' merge is bounded (:397). See Question 1.
- Capacity overflows answer FAILURE (chip's `CHIP_ERROR_NO_MEMORY`), not RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED. The multicast policy of a key set is not copied, because chip's `CopyGroupKeySetWithOwnedSpans` does not copy it.
- The fail-safe check of ICACCSRRequest and AddICAC is the node's (`armed()`), not per accessing fabric (`IsFailSafeArmed(fabricIndex)`), as in 26a.
- `AnnounceJointFabricAdministrator` holds the invoke until the verification ends. chip sends a deferred response; the wire behaviour is the same. Without a `CommissionerReach` it answers FAILURE. There is no node adapter over the client stack (Question 2).
- Both roles look up the trust anchor with chip's placeholder (the other side's RCAC, `JCMCommissionee.cpp:71-81`, `PairingCommand.cpp:935-946`) unless the host gives a lookup.
- The anchor detection at CommissioningComplete is the example application's behaviour, now in the server (Question 3).
- The commissioner reports one flow step, 10.2. The JCM stages reach `JointFabricOptions.Progress`, not `Options.Progress`.
- chip's `HasValidCommissioningMode` (CM=3 required under JCM, `DeviceCommissioner.cpp:529-551`) is not ported. The Go commissioner judges no CM in its discovery data.

## Decisions

- `client/jcm` is the shared package, in the model-and-protocol layer as client machinery of both roles. The commissioner does not import `cluster/jointfabric`.
- Port and Host names: `DatastoreSync` (field `Sync`), `DatastoreStore` (field `Store`), `AdministratorPort`, `CommissionerReach`. `Options.JointFabric`, `Options.CATs` and `commissioner.TrustInfo` follow the brief.
- The node attaches its store and its commissioning window to `AttachNode` participants, after the fail-safe pattern. `wire.OpenWindowParams.JointFabric` carries the joint flag to the bridge.

## Left out

- chip's 4 `VendorIdVerificationClientLiveness` tests (C++ lifetime guard), replaced by one context test.
- An over-the-wire check of the CM=3 advertisement (TC_JFADMIN_2_2 step 4); the flag is set and read in unit code.
- A node adapter for `CommissionerReach`.
- The commissioner's CM=3 check.

## Questions

1. **Refresh binding merge.** chip's `ContinueRefresh` (`JointFabricDatastore.cpp:567-585`) drops a node's committed bindings on every other endpoint while it merges one endpoint's fetched list. The port bounds the removal to the refreshed endpoint, as the groups' merge does (:397). Keep the bounded merge as a recorded divergence, or mirror chip's predicate verbatim?
2. **CommissionerReach in the node.** The administrator's reads of the announcing commissioner are a host port. Should a follow-up give the node an adapter over its client stack (`node.Client`, `interaction.ForPeer`, signing on the peer's CASE session), so the kit works without host code?
3. **Anchor at CommissioningComplete.** The Administrator takes the fabric whose NOC carries the administrator and anchor CATs as AdministratorFabricIndex and records it in the Datastore, as chip's example application does (`JFAManager.cpp:95-157`). chip's server does not do this. Keep it in the server, or leave it to the host?

## by_design candidates

1. **BD-chip-JointFabricDatastore-RulesInServer.** matter.js `JointFabricDatastoreServer.ts:14` at `862ad75f` is the generated behaviour. `cluster/jointfabric.Datastore` follows connectedhomeip `JointFabricDatastore.cpp` at `6170af84`: the operations and their status codes (:181-2373), the refresh (:241-1130), the stable-key commit (`.h:75-85`) and the 20 commands (`joint-fabric-datastore-server.cpp:293-785`). The delegate becomes the synchronous `DatastoreSync`; a command answers before its push, and a failed push leaves the entry Pending. Every changed attribute is reported, where chip reports NodeList only (:287-291). The tables persist in the TLS servers' shape, where chip keeps them in memory (`.h:562`). Without the rules, a controller could not keep a Joint Fabric's registry.
2. **BD-chip-JointFabricAdministrator-RulesInServer.** matter.js `JointFabricAdministratorServer.ts:14` is the generated behaviour. `cluster/jointfabric.Administrator` follows `joint-fabric-administrator-server.cpp:172-449` and `JCMCommissionee.cpp` behind `AdministratorPort` and `CommissionerReach`, with `client/jcm` after `src/credentials/jcm`. TransferAnchorRequest and TransferAnchorComplete answer SUCCESS without effect, because chip's handlers are empty (:439-449). The fail-safe check is the node's. Without the rules, the administrator's ICAC exchange and its trust verification have no checks.
3. **BD-chip-JCM-Commissioner.** matter.js's commissioning flow has no Joint Commissioning step (`ControllerCommissioningFlow.ts:573-585`). The commissioner runs chip's (`src/controller/jcm/DeviceCommissioner.cpp`) as step 10.2 `JointFabric.TrustVerification`, numbered after DeviceAttestation 10.1, where chip runs `kJCMTrustVerification` after attestation (`AutoCommissioner.cpp:522-531`). The stages report through `JointFabricOptions.Progress`. Without it, a commissioner cannot vet a joining administrator before issuing its NOC.
4. **BD-chip-JF-RefreshBindingMergePerEndpoint** (if Question 1 keeps it). The refresh's binding merge drops only the refreshed endpoint's missing committed bindings. chip's predicate (`JointFabricDatastore.cpp:567-585`) drops every other endpoint's as well, which a refresh of a node with bindings on two endpoints shows.
5. **BD-chip-JF-RefreshOfUnknownNode.** A refresh of a node that is not held ends the refresh. chip leaves `mRefreshingNodeId` set (:247-249) and refuses every later refresh.
6. **BD-chip-JF-AnchorAtCommissioningComplete** (if Question 3 keeps it). The Administrator takes the administrator-and-anchor fabric at CommissioningComplete, as `JFAManager.cpp:95-157` does in chip's example application, so that a kit device records its anchor without host code.
