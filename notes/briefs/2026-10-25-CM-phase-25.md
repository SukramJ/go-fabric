## 1 OTA push from the controller (matter.js `software-update/`)

The provider half already exists on the device stack (`node.OTAProviderConfig`,
`node/ota_provider.go`, `bdx/`); the reference CLI runs its own
`node.Node` to reach it (`examples/reference-controller/cli/self.go:119, :184-193`).
matter.js runs the provider on the controller's own node and drives it
with three pieces; port them into a new package `controller/softwareupdate`
(name after ADR 0025: spec-near, one shape):

- **1.1 `OtaAnnouncements`** (`packages/node/src/behavior/system/software-update/OtaAnnouncements.ts`
  at `862ad75f`): the queue of peers, the 10 s per-peer delay (`:54-57`),
  `#announceOtaProvider` (`:159-215`: skip when not commissioned or not
  online `:160-163`; find the requestor endpoint `:221`, `:257`; write the
  own-fabric `DefaultOTAProviders` record only when absent or different
  `:175-197`; the 24 h retry notice `:208-211`; **no automatic Simple
  Announcement**, by matter.js's decision `:200-203` — a `by_design`
  candidate if the spec text says otherwise), `announceOtaProvider(reason)`
  (`:266-292`, `ClosedError` → retry later).
- **1.2 `SoftwareUpdateManager`** (`SoftwareUpdateManager.ts`, 1 444 lines):
  the update queue and its entries (`:79-139`), `OtaUpdateStatus` (`:105`),
  the consent model (`addUpdateConsent`, `consentRequired` `:431-457`,
  matching by vendor/product/version `:419-421`), `checkForUpdates`/`queryUpdates`

  (`client/peer/reboot_armer.go`) finally gets its caller. Status

- **1.3 The provider server on the controller's node** (`OtaSoftwareUpdateProviderServer.ts:91-515`):
  `queryImage` (`:163-399`: NotAvailable when no update `:168-174`, the
