## Decisions

1. **One public shape: `node.Network.WiFi` is the node's Wi-Fi interface.** `WiFi` gains `Driver contract.WiFiDriver`: non-nil mounts NetworkCommissioning with the Wi-Fi feature in place of the Ethernet one (what the seam did); `Reporter` keeps mounting WiFiNetworkDiagnostics. Either or both; a non-nil `WiFi` with neither is the validation error. The seam goes without a forwarder: `nodeseam.UseWiFiCommissioning`, the unexported field and type, and `fabrictest.UseWiFiCommissioning` are removed, their users set `cfg.Network.WiFi` (a test that also has a reporter merges the two). The "TESTING ONLY" and "the module's nodes are Ethernet-attached" sentences leave the comments; `contract/wifi.go` names the real driver.
2. **A join is announced at once.** After a successful `ConnectNetwork` — the command's and the rejoin at start — the node calls `TriggerReannounce`, as matter.js's example does after its connect (`examples/device-onoff-advanced/src/cluster/DummyWifiNetworkCommissioningServer.ts:132-133`, "Announce operational in IP network"). Do it in `node` around the driver it hands the cluster; the cluster does not change.
3. **`wifi/wpasupplicant` is a nested module** (`github.com/SukramJ/go-fabric/wifi/wpasupplicant`, godbus, no cgo) with one constructor: `wpasupplicant.New(ctx, Options{Interface string; ScanTimeout, ConnectTimeout time.Duration; Logger *slog.Logger}) (*Driver, error)` and `(*Driver).Close() error`; `*Driver` implements `contract.WiFiDriver`. Defaults: interface `wlan0`, 10 s, 20 s (the cluster's maxima). Off Linux `New` fails with an error wrapping `errors.ErrUnsupported`. It follows connectedhomeip's Linux driver — matter.js has no Wi-Fi driver (`NetworkCommissioningServer.ts:18` is an empty class):
   - **Bring-up** (WPA `:464-520`, `:453-461`, `:1232-1246`): service `fi.w1.wpa_supplicant1` at `/fi/w1/wpa_supplicant1` on the system bus (`:63-64`); `GetInterface(ifname)`, on failure `CreateInterface({"Ifname": ifname})`; then auto scan is switched off (`AutoScan("")`, "or it will block our network scan"), a failure of that logged, not fatal.
   - **Scan** (WPA `:1251-1280`, `:1545-1595`, `_GetBssInfo` `:1385-1543`): one scan at a time (a second is UnknownError); `Scan({"Type": "active"})`; wait for the interface's scan-done signal or the timeout; read `BSSs`; per BSS `SSID`, `BSSID`, `Signal`, `Frequency`, `WPA`, `RSN`. A BSS that is gone, an SSID over 32 bytes or a BSSID not of 6 bytes is dropped; `Signal` is clamped into int8 (`:1516-1527`); band and channel come from chip's frequency table, ported whole (`:1286-1380`); the security bitmap from the `KeyMgmt` lists as chip maps them (`:1437-1507` — read it: WPA-PSK, WPA2-PSK variants, SAE → WPA3, EAP-only is not reported as open, nothing → Unencrypted, `:1533-1538`); a directed scan filters by SSID (`:1568-1573`).
   - **Connect** (WPA `:914-943`, `:852-911`, `:287-418`): one at a time (`:932`); state `interface_disabled` fails (`WpaSupplicantClient.cpp:209-214`); a `CurrentNetwork` other than `/` is removed first (`:861-877`); `AddNetwork({ssid, psk, key_mgmt: "SAE WPA-PSK"})` (`:936-940`); `Disconnect`, errors ignored (`:892-895`); `SelectNetwork` (`:897-904`). The verdict comes from the interface's `State`: `associating` marks the attempt started; `completed` after that is Success; `disconnected` after that is the failure — unless `AssocStatusCode` is `WLAN_STATUS_AUTH_TIMEOUT` and retries are left (8, `:56-61`), then `SelectNetwork` again (`:324-348`) — reported as UnknownError with `errorValue` = the interface's `DisconnectReason` (`:303`, `:378-379`). The context's end or the timeout is UnknownError with `errorValue` 0. Timers through a clock seam or the context; no `time.Sleep`/`time.After`.
   - **Signals:** the `State` changes are read from `org.freedesktop.DBus.Properties.PropertiesChanged` for the interface `fi.w1.wpa_supplicant1.Interface` (chip's `g-properties-changed`, WPA `:434-438`), not from wpa_supplicant's own `PropertiesChanged` signal; the scan's end is the interface's `ScanDone` (`:440-444`). A property the service does not serve reads as 0 — connectedhomeip's test mock (`src/python_testing/matter_testing_infrastructure/matter/testing/linux/wifi.py:321-697`), which 28c runs the driver against, has neither `DisconnectReason` nor `AssocStatusCode`, answers `SelectNetwork` with a scan before it walks `associating`, `associated`, `completed` (`:419-438`), and reads nothing but the calls' shapes.
   - **SSID bytes:** chip hands `ssid` as a D-Bus string. Do the same for an SSID that is valid UTF-8 without NUL; for any other SSID use the byte-array form if wpa_supplicant's D-Bus documentation (w1.fi, `doc/dbus.doxygen`) allows it — cite it — else refuse with UnknownError and a debug text, and report.
   - **Open networks:** empty credentials are `key_mgmt: "NONE"` without `psk`. Chip sends `psk ""` there; the cluster accepts the empty credential (`validCredentials`), so the driver joins. `by_design` candidate — name it in the report.
   - **Not mirrored, on purpose** (`by_design` candidates, text by Fable): no `SaveConfig` (WPA `:1118-1135` through `CommitConfiguration`, `NetworkCommissioningWiFiDriver.cpp:135-167`) — the committed network lives in the node's store and `Start` rejoins, so the credential is in one place; no DHCP client (`PostNetworkConnect`, the `CHIP_DEVICE_CONFIG_LINUX_DHCPC_CMD` block, WPA `:1098-1115`) — addressing is the host's network stack's, Matter needs the IPv6 link-local address the kernel configures; no PDC, no AP mode, no Wi-Fi PAF.
   - **The credential is never logged** and never part of a debug text or an error; the fake bus sees it in `AddNetwork` only, and a test holds that against the log output.
4. **The example is a module of its own: `examples/ble-wifi-light`** (own `go.mod` requiring the main module, `ble/bluez` and `wifi/wpasupplicant`, each with a `replace` into the repository; not tagged — an example is built from a clone). One on/off light through the `node` and `device` facade (`examples/quickstart-device` is the pattern), `bluez.NewPeripheral` as `Network.BLE`, `wpasupplicant.New` as `Network.WiFi.Driver`, the sqlite store in `--storage`, flags for adapter, Wi-Fi interface, passcode, discriminator; prints the QR code and the manual code. It is what 28c drives under chip's BlueZ and wpa_supplicant mocks and what the owner runs on a Pi. The reference daemon gets no BLE flag: the main module does not import godbus, and neither library module imports the other.
5. **Tooling:** `make wpasupplicant-check` (the module, as `bluez-check`) and the example's build, vet, lint, `go mod tidy -diff` — one target or two, both in `make ci`; a CI job `wifi/wpasupplicant` in `ci.yml` running them; Dependabot entries for both directories (ignore the in-repo modules, as the existing entries do); `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` and `licenses/` of the module; the root `README.md` module table; `docs/` — the Wi-Fi section beside 28a's BLE section in `docs/guides/operations.md` and `docs/guides/ports.md`: wpa_supplicant must run with its D-Bus interface, the interface must not be managed by NetworkManager at the same time, the process needs the system bus's permission for `fi.w1.wpa_supplicant1`, addressing is the host's.

## Tests (each fails before the change)

- `wifi/wpasupplicant` over a fake bus that plays wpa_supplicant: bring-up by `GetInterface`, and by `CreateInterface` when that fails; a scan's results with clamped signal, band and channel for 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz frequencies, each security mapping, a dropped malformed BSS, the SSID filter; a second scan refused; the scan timeout. Connect: the call order (remove the current network, add, disconnect, select); Success at `completed` only after `associating`; a `disconnected` with its reason as `errorValue`; the auth-timeout retry up to 8 and then the failure; `interface_disabled`; the timeout; a second connect refused; an open network; a non-UTF-8 SSID; the credential absent from every log line. `Close` ends a waiting call.
- `node`: `Network.WiFi{Driver}` mounts the Wi-Fi NetworkCommissioning (the seam's tests moved onto it); `WiFi{}` is the validation error; a successful connect triggers the re-announcement (the command and the rejoin), a failed one does not.
- `fabrictest` and the commissioner's BLE tests run unchanged in behaviour on the public field.
- The example builds for linux/amd64, linux/arm64 and linux/arm; a test starts it with in-process fakes if its `main` is cut for that, otherwise say what covers it (28c drives the binary).

## Better than the others, than before, the right cut

- **Than matter.js:** it ships no Wi-Fi driver at all — its example fakes the join (`DummyWifiNetworkCommissioningServer.ts:110-139`); a host gets a working one here. **Than chip:** the same wpa_supplicant calls without GLib and cgo, behind a two-method port instead of a platform singleton; the credential is stored once, in the node's store, not also in wpa_supplicant's configuration file.
- **Than before:** Wi-Fi commissioning leaves the test seam and becomes the facade's; `Network.BLE` and `Network.WiFi` state a wireless node in two fields.
- **The cut:** the cluster owns the Matter rules, the port owns the radio, the driver is a module a wired host never pulls, and the example composes the two radio modules without either knowing the other.
