    ADR 0020 — The commissioner and controller role enters the scope as controller/; the responder side stays as it is¶

    • Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-10-13; proposed the same day; concept v2 ../concept-v2-scope-extension.md decision 0.1, phase 7). The non-goal it supersedes is retired; phase 7 starts with stage 1.
    • Date: 2026-10-13
    • Related: ADR 0008 — subscription resumption (the narrow CASE initiator), ADR 0011 — certifiability is a goal, ADR 0018 — device layer and node facade, ADR 0019 — TCP, BDX and the OTA requestor, ../matterjs-comparison.md §1–§3 and §Non-goals, concept v1 ../concept-matter-1.6.1-and-device-layer.md §F.1, notes/parity/by_design.md BD-Matter-GroupcastNoSender, BD-Matter-Commissioner-TestCA (the test commissioner's fixed issuing instant)

    Context¶

    Four places record "no controller / commissioner role" as a decision, not backlog: CLAUDE.md §"Scope boundaries", docs/matterjs-comparison.md §Non-goals item 1, ADR 0008 (which carved out the one bounded exception — the device re-establishing its own former subscriptions opens a CASE session as the initiator and does nothing else), and concept v1 §F.1 ("a different product, not an extension"). The reasoning was that a bridge answers; a second state machine, a certificate-issuer role and a device cache serve no bridge.

    Concept v2 (2026-10-12, decision 0.1) reverses that for two consumers that did not exist when the non-goal was written:

    1. A controller daemon as a product outside this module (working title go-matter-controller, the same relationship the reference daemon has to node/): it commissions nodes, keeps their sessions, subscribes and offers the devices to a host.
    2. The certification harness. internal/chiptool commissions the reference daemon with chip-tool and with matter.js's controller; the BIND family and every "controller" case need a third party that the harness can drive. A commissioner of our own replaces both for those cases and proves the role the way the responder is proved.

    What exists today is a responder with the initiator's wire pieces already in place: secure/sigma.NewPeerInitiator and bridge/case_initiator.go (ADR 0008), bridge/bridgetest.Commissioner (#53: PASE, ArmFailSafe, attestation and CSR requests, AddTrustedRootCertificate, AddNOC, CASE, CommissioningComplete in chip-tool's AutoCommissioner order, over a test CA), secure/mattercert (the certificate codec), secure/attestation (the device side), mdns/ (an advertiser and an operational resolver, no browser), commissioning/ (the responder side of PASE). matter.js's controller is packages/protocol/src/peer/ (PeerSet, Peer, PeerConnection, ControllerCommissioner, ControllerCommissioningFlow, CommissioningConnection), packages/protocol/src/mdns/CommissionableMdnsScanner.ts and common/Scanner.ts, session/pase/PaseClient.ts, session/case/CaseClient.ts, packages/node/src/node/ClientNode.ts and node/client/, behaviors/binding/, the sender half of behaviors/groupcast/GroupcastServer.ts, the Joint Fabric behaviours and protocol/src/dcl/ — about 22 000 lines at 0ceb202d, measured in concept v2 §B.1.

    Decision¶

    The controller and commissioner role is in scope, as a package of its own, ported from matter.js in the six stages of concept v2 §B.1. The boundary:

    1. controller/ is a sibling of bridge/, not part of it. It imports secure/, tlv/, im/ (the codecs), mdns/, transport/ and schema/, and owns its state machine, its peer table, its certificate authority and its client-side interaction. bridge/, node/, device/ and endpoint/ do not import it and are not changed for it. A host that embeds the responder keeps a module whose controller code is dead weight the linker drops.
    2. contract/ stays stdlib-only and stays the responder's host boundary. The controller's host-facing surface is its own (controller/ types; a later ADR if it needs a contract package).
    3. The shared packages are now used by two roles. secure/, tlv/, im/, mdns/, transport/ carry the responder's parity guards and the chip-tool suite; every change made for the controller runs through those guards first, and a divergence a role needs is a by_design entry naming the role. The initiator paths of secure/sigma and the mDNS browser are the first such shared code; ADR 0008's initiator becomes the first user of controller/'s session client rather than a special case in bridge/.
    4. On-network only. Nodes are discovered and commissioned over IP (DNS-SD _matterc._udp, the way chip-tool pairing onnetwork and matter.js's CommissionableMdnsScanner do). No BLE, no Thread, no Wi-Fi commissioning over BLE — those non-goals stand (concept v2 decisions 0.4, 0.6).
    5. The certificate authority is the package's default CA, grown from bridgetest's test CA (stage 2; since wave 16 bridgetest mints through it, its fixed issuing instant staying by_design). Attestation is verified against a PAA store the host supplies, with the CSA test PAAs as the default for the harness. The DCL client (stage 6) is an HTTP client against the CSA ledger over net/http; it is the last stage and separable.
    6. No device model of its own in the controller. The client view of a remote node comes from schema/ and the device/ types (stage 4, the port of ClientNode / ClientStructure); the controller daemon projects it to its host, as the reference daemon projects the other way.
    7. matter.js stays the single gold standard for the controller as for the responder; chip-tool is the authority on tool behaviour the harness compares against (its argument handling, its output), not a second reference for the protocol. Every // Mirrors matter.js … comment, parity test and by_design rule applies unchanged.

    Consequences¶

    • CLAUDE.md §"Scope boundaries", docs/matterjs-comparison.md §Non-goals item 1, ADR 0008 and concept v1 §F.1 carry a note that this ADR supersedes the non-goal; the text they keep is history. The comparison's §1–§3 "Controller" rows move from No to the stage that ports them when the stage lands, not before.
    • v1.0.0 moves behind phases 7–9 (concept v2 decision 0.11): the freeze then covers device/, node/ and controller/ together; consumers stay on v0.x guarantees meanwhile (README §API stability).
    • The reachability inventory and the coverage floors gain a package; the chip-tool CI gains a leg in which controller/ is the commissioner (chiptool.yml), beside the chip-tool and matter.js-controller legs, which stay — the control leg's purpose (separating our defects from the environment's) needs a commissioner that is not ours.
    • secure/sigma and mdns/ get initiator- and browser-side parity tests of their own (PaseClient.ts, CaseClient.ts, CommissionableMdnsScanner.ts fixtures); the responder fixtures are not touched.
    • Not decided here: the controller daemon's own host contract, the persistence of peers and the CA key (a store/ extension or the daemon's own), and whether the DCL client lives in controller/ or beside it. Each is a later ADR when its stage starts.
